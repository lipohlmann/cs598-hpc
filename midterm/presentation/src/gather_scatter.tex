\begin{frame}{Gather-Scatter Operator}
  As discussed in \cite{devilleHighorderMethodsIncompressible2002}, the \textit{scatter} operator copies node values on element boundaries, while the \textit{gather} operator sums these values together.
  This allows for a single sharing of values after each iteration and updating of all nodes.
  \begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
       & \mathbf{u}_L & = & Q\mathbf{u}     & \text{scatter} \\
       & \mathbf{v}   & = & Q^T\mathbf{u}_L & \text{gather}
    \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}
  This was handled using \texttt{gslib} (\url{https://github.com/nek5000/gslib}).
\end{frame}
